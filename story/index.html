Sources

The maps, radargrams, model results and temperature curves on this page are drawn from data; the code that prepares them is in story/scripts/ of the PHIRE repository.

  • Radar. DELORES ice-penetrating radar, Henry Ice Rise, 2013/14 and 2014/15 field seasons (British Antarctic Survey). Track order and dates from the radar acquisition logs. The 9 January 2014 line along the ridge is the ReflexW export, re-referenced to a smoothed GPS surface; HEnorth FL5 as processed for Kingslake et al. (2018). Display gain applied here.
  • Interpretation. Kingslake, J., et al. (2018). Extensive retreat and re-advance of the West Antarctic Ice Sheet during the Holocene. Nature 558, 430–434. doi:10.1038/s41586-018-0208-x. Wearing, M. G., and Kingslake, J. (2019). Holocene formation of Henry Ice Rise, West Antarctica, inferred from ice-penetrating radar. JGR Earth Surface.
  • Ice-sheet model. Reference simulation (2205) of Kingslake et al. (2018): Albrecht, T. (2018). PISM simulation results of the Antarctic Ice Sheet deglaciation. GFZ Data Services. doi:10.5880/PIK.2018.008 (CC BY 4.0). Grounded-ice outlines every 200 years, 15 km grid.
  • Temperature profiles. 1-D model of a 500 m column after grounding, from preliminary-planning/vertical-temperature-after-grounding.ipynb. Site values are placeholders.
  • Imagery. Landsat Image Mosaic of Antarctica (LIMA; USGS, NASA, BAS, NSF) and MODIS Mosaic of Antarctica (NSIDC), via Quantarctica; contrast-enhanced. WorldView-1 image of 28 February 2025 © Maxar Technologies, via the Polar Geospatial Center.
  • Ice boundaries and speed. MEaSUREs Antarctic Boundaries and MEaSUREs ice velocity (NSIDC), via Quantarctica.

Maps are in polar stereographic projection (EPSG:3031). 3-D view vertically exaggerated three times. A one-minute version covers just the two radar seasons and the drilling.
